## Description

Measure of carbon emissions (CO2e) for a period of time by gigabytes of storage capacity. This metric helps organizations understand the environmental impact of cloud storage usage and make decisions to reduce their carbon footprint by choosing more efficient or renewable-energy-powered storage options.
