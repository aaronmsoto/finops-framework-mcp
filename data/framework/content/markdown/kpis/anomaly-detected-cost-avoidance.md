## Description

Measures amount of cost avoidance as a result of identifying and correcting an anomaly. [See Playbook for more details](https://www.finops.org/wg/measuring-anomaly-detected-cost-avoidance-playbook/).

## Formula

```
Total cost of the unpredicted & correctable amount of spend * Projected Amount of Days the anomaly could have occurred if the underlying issue was not addressed
```
