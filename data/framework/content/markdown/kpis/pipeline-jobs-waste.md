## Description

In the context of public cloud, the terms “jobs” and “pipelines” often refer to specific cloud services designed for automation and orchestration.

This is a quantitative measure for understanding “jobs waste” in the context of cloud services. It quantifies the proportion of total job costs that were attributed to jobs that didn’t complete successfully (failed, errored, cancelled, or timed out).

To effectively use this formula, you would need:

1. Cost data for each job: This would typically come from the billing or cost management tools provided by the cloud vendor.
2. Status of each job: You’d need to know whether each job ended in a “Failed,” “Error,” “Cancelled,” “Timed_out,” or a successful state.

 

Failed jobs are sunk costs which represent waste to an organization. The intent is to lower your waste percentage in production.
