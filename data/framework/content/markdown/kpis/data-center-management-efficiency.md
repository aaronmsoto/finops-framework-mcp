## Description

Measures the cost efficiency of the human resources required to manage data center operations relative to the value of the infrastructure being managed. The formula calculates the total full-time equivalent labor cost, multiplied by their hourly rate, and divides it by the total value of the managed infrastructure. A lower Operational Load Factor indicates that the data center is being managed efficiently with less human resource overhead per unit of infrastructure value. This KPI was developed by the [FinOps for Data Center Working Group](https://www.finops.org/wg/finops-for-data-center-creating-a-finops-practice-profile/#acknowledgments).
