## Description

Measure of carbon emissions (CO2e) per unit of cloud spend. Ideally, this measure would be drillable by scope and service categories, such as compute, storage, or networking.

This metric helps organizations understand the environmental impact of cloud usage and make decisions to reduce their carbon footprint by choosing more efficient or renewable-energy-powered cloud services.

## Formula

```
CO2e/unit of currency = Effective Cost / CO2e
```
