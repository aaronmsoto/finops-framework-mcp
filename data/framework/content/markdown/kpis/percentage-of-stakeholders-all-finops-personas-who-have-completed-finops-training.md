## Description

Percentage of all FinOps-related personas and resources who have completed training and/or certification. This includes internal and/or external training and certification.

## Formula

```
(Target for certification – actual count certified ) / target for certification * 100
```

## Candidate Data Sources

- Credly
- LinkedIn
- Employee Surveys
- Self Reporting
- Internal Training / Development
