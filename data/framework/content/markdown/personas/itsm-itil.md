## Description

IT Service Management, ITIL, or similar service management disciplines may exist in organizations which adopt these frameworks. Service management teams may maintain Configuration Management Databases (CMDB) or other service management or operational data which can be leveraged by the FinOps team to understand how to allocate costs, how to tag resources, or other operational aspects of the cloud infrastructure.
