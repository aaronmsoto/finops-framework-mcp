## Key takeaways {section=key-takeaways}

- Three in four enterprises cannot show their CFO value on their AI spending. Only 7% named price as their biggest challenge.
- The mix is shifting: 83% expect to move more toward open weights models over the next 12 months, while still running frontier.
- Model routing is being run or evaluated by 86% of organizations, and the biggest enterprises are building and buying routers at once.
- Ownership matters. Organizations with a defined owner were 3.7x more likely to show value.
- Mission critical AI cannot simply be switched off, which pushes AI OpEx back toward capacity planning.
